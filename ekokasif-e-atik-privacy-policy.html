Privacy Policy

Ekokaşif E-Waste
Effective date: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ekokaşif E-Waste (the “App”) handles information when you use the application.

Summary: The App is designed to provide an educational and game-based e-waste recycling experience. It does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information from users.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information through the App. The App does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, telephone number, postal address, precise location, contacts, photographs, or other identifying information to use its core features.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores game progress and preferences locally on your device using the device's local storage. This may include information such as points, stars, experience, level, discovered e-waste items, sorting progress, quiz progress, unlocked garden items, badges, daily quest progress, sound preferences, and an optional in-app name if one is entered.

This information is used only to provide the App's gameplay and personalization features. It is not intentionally transmitted to us or associated with an online account.

3. Optional Name / Profile Information

The App may allow a user to enter a name for personalization within the game. If provided, this name is stored locally on the device as part of the App's game data. We do not intentionally transmit this information to our servers or use it for advertising.

4. Audio and Game Sounds

The App uses audio features and locally generated game sounds for gameplay. These sounds are produced on the device. The App does not require microphone access for its core functionality and does not intentionally record or transmit microphone audio.

5. Children's Privacy

Ekokaşif E-Waste is an educational game designed to make e-waste recycling and environmental awareness engaging for children and families.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. The App does not require children to create an account or submit personal information in order to access its core educational and game features.

6. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The App does not intentionally include third-party advertising, behavioral advertising, or cross-app tracking.

The App's current source code does not intentionally send gameplay progress or personal information to an analytics service. We do not sell personal information.

7. Third-Party Services and Resources

The App may use standard third-party web resources, such as Google Fonts, to display its user interface correctly. When such external resources are requested, the relevant provider may receive standard technical information associated with the network request, such as an IP address. Such processing is governed by the provider's own privacy practices.

Apart from standard external resources described above, the App's core gameplay data is designed to remain on the user's device.

8. Permissions

The App does not require access to contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone for its core functionality.

9. Data Security

Because the App does not require an online account and does not intentionally collect personal information on our servers, we do not maintain a server-side personal profile for App users. Locally stored App data is protected by the security mechanisms of the user's device and Android operating system.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain server-side user accounts for the App. Locally stored game data can generally be deleted by clearing the App's storage/data in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's functionality, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published at the applicable privacy-policy location and will include a revised effective date.
